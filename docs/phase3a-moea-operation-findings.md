# Phase 3A MOEA operation-survey findings

Source: https://service.moea.gov.tw/EE520/investigate/InvestigateEC.aspx?lang=E

The official MOEA Manufacturing Investment & Operation Overview Survey is quarterly/yearly rather than daily or monthly. Its selectable statistical items include manufacturing operating revenue and purchase of fixed assets; the industry tree includes (25) Manufacture of Fabricated Metal Products, (24) Manufacture of Basic Metals, (29) Manufacture of Machinery and Equipment, and other manufacturing sectors.

This source is suitable only as a quarterly capacity/operation proxy if the implementation can obtain a stable bounded query. It must not be used as a supplier quotation or per-piece/per-kg/per-hour fabrication price. Its quarterly frequency requires quarterly or annual comparison windows; it must not be forced into weekly momentum.
