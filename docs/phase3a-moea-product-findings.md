# Phase 3A MOEA product-statistics findings

Source: https://service.moea.gov.tw/EE520/investigate/InvestigateDA.aspx?lang=E

The official MOEA Product Statistics page exposes monthly, quarterly, yearly, and mixed-period options. It provides Production Volume, Production Value, Sales Volume, Sales Value, Domestic Sales Volume/Value, Direct Export Volume/Value, Inventory Volume, and Inventory Value.

The page has a product-group search and detailed industry tree. It is an official source suitable for demand/capacity or activity proxies when queried through a bounded stable interface. It is not a supplier price source. Any implementation must preserve the measure, product group, date range, unit, and source status and must not convert production or sales statistics into sheet-metal quotation prices.
