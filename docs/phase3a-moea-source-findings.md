# Phase 3A MOEA source findings

Source: https://service.moea.gov.tw/EE520/investigate/InvestigateDB.aspx?lang=E

The official Ministry of Economic Affairs Industrial Production, Shipment & Inventory Statistics Survey exposes monthly, quarterly, yearly, and mixed period options. It offers measures including indexes of industrial production, producer shipments, producer inventory, production value, sales value, domestic sales value, export value, inventory value, and inventory ratio.

The industry tree visibly includes Manufacturing, Metal & Machinery Industry, Manufacture of Basic Metals, iron and steel subgroups, aluminum subgroups, copper subgroups, Manufacture of Fabricated Metal Products, Manufacture of Cutlery and Metal Hand Tools, Manufacture of Metal Die, and additional fabricated-metal subgroups below the viewport. This is a credible Taiwan-first public source for demand/capacity pressure proxies, not supplier pricing.

The page is an interactive Web Forms selector; any implementation should use a bounded official query or a stable public export only after confirming the request contract. Values must remain OBSERVED_PUBLIC_DATA and must not be interpreted as per-piece, per-kg, or per-hour sheet-metal quotations.
